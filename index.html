Moonbow share, Archive strains

Moonbow = Zkittlez x Dosidos. Expected % of genome from the tree (50% per parent, 100% through a selection). Non-Archive strains are 0% except Icy OG, Fish Sticks and Truffle Alert, which get only distant Zkittlez or Dosidos.

Within Archive
