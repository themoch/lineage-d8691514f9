Shared ancestors that matter

  • Moonbow 112 F2 #60 (one male) sits behind 11 of your 15: Oishii, Ichibo and Moonbow 112 IX directly, and everything built on Oishii.
  • Oishii is a parent or grandparent of 9 of the other 14: Ichiban, Bingo!, Circle Jerk, Zwoosh/Zumo, Nubo, Wraith, Nu Walka, Lemon Venom, Ichibo.

Closest pairs (line-breeding territory)

  • Ichibo x Ichiban, Moonbow 112 IX x Ichibo, Moonbow 112 IX x Dream Machine / Crazy Hazey: about 69%

Best outcross options

  • Biscotti shares only deep GSC / OG Kush roots with the rest. Biscotti x Moonbow 112 IX, Oishii or Ichibo gives the most hybrid vigor.
  • Nu Walka and Wraith are only about 9% Moonbow. Strong partners for the Moonbow-heavy group (Moonbow 112 IX, Oishii, Ichibo, Crazy Hazey).
  • Lowest overlap overall: Nu Walka x Biscotti, then Nu Walka x Dream Machine / Crazy Hazey / Moonbow 112 IX.

Caveat: Nu Diesel, DogWalker OG, Super Runtz, Albert Walker and Lemon Peel have no published parents. If any carry OG Kush or Zkittlez (likely for some), real overlap is higher than shown.
