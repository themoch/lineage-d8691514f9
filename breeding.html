Bridges between your Archive and non-Archive strains

  • OGKB / GSC is the big shared root. It runs through all 15 Archive strains and also Truffle Alert, Cross of the Titans (via Mendo Breath and Grateful Breath) and Sour Trop (via Tropicana Cookies).
  • Zkittlez links Icy OG (via Icy Grape) to the Archive Moonbow line, so Fish Sticks carries a little Archive-adjacent background.
  • Dosidos shows up outside Archive in Truffle Alert, via White Truffle → Gorilla Butter → Peanut Butter Breath.
  • Chemdog ties Sour Trop, Cross of the Titans, Watermelon Hashplant and the (GG4 x I-95) x Chemdog S1.
  • GG4 ties (GG4 x I-95) x Chemdog S1, Truffle Alert and Money Tree, and now Wedding Glue Auto (Wedding Cake x Gorilla Glue). That last link is dashed because the note doesn't say whether it is GG4 or another Gorilla Glue cut.
  • Wedding Cake ties Wedding Glue Auto to Archive's Dosi-Cake. It is the only Archive link among the newest strains, and Wedding Cake's own parentage is disputed, so it stops there.
  • Cinderella 99 ties Golden Pineapple, Golden PuTang and Money Tree (via Jilly Bean → Space Queen).
  • Blueberry ties Diablo to Blue Tuna (via Blue Dream) and so to Fish Sticks. Diablo's Grapefruit side is the same Grapefruit behind Zkittlez, a faint link to the Archive Moonbow line and Icy OG.
  • Thai / Afghani (inside Blueberry) also tie Diablo to Star Pupil, so Golden PuTang and Valedictorian.
  • Star Pupil / Pakistani tie Golden PuTang and Valedictorian. Tangie ties Golden PuTang and Sour Trop.

Your own crosses

  • Big Bud ties Salmon Creek Big Bud to Salmon Creek Big Bud F2. Both come from the same Mendocino line and share nothing with the rest of the collection.
  • Fish Sticks (Blue Tuna x Icy OG): the parents share no known ancestors. That is a true outcross, so expect a lot of variation in the F1 and vigor.
  • Bubble PAK (Bubble Gum x Medi Pak): also a clean outcross. Bubble Gum candy plus Pakistani hashplant structure.
  • Diablo x Fish Sticks would line-breed Blueberry (Diablo is 75% Blueberry, Fish Sticks about 12%). Diablo x Bubble PAK is a clean outcross.
  • Goldfish (Kodiak Gold x Black Tuna) is a clean outcross to everything you grow. Its Black Tuna (Herijuana x Lamb's Bread) is not related to Blue Tuna's Tuna Kush as far as anything published shows, despite the name. Cherry Wine F2 is the same story: a CBD line with no known ancestors in common with the rest of the collection.
  • Wedding Glue Auto is an autoflower, so crossing it passes on the autoflowering trait only if both parents carry it. Expect a mostly photoperiod line from any cross to your other strains.
  • Most different from the Archive block: Laos Landrace, Salmon Creek Big Bud and its F2, Blue Tuna, Bubble PAK, Medi Pak, Valedictorian, Meigs OG, Goldfish, Cherry Wine F2. None share a known ancestor with the Archive strains.

Moonbow share, Archive strains

Moonbow = Zkittlez x Dosidos. Expected % of genome from the tree (50% per parent, 100% through a selection). Non-Archive strains are 0% except Icy OG, Fish Sticks and Truffle Alert, which get only distant Zkittlez or Dosidos.

Within Archive

  • Dosi-Cake is 50% Dosidos but 0% Moonbow. It shares Dosidos (and OGKB / Face Off) with the whole Archive block but none of the Moonbow / Oishii stack, so it is a mid-distance cross for the Moonbow-heavy strains.
  • Moonbow 112 F2 #60 (one male) sits behind 11 of the 15 Archive strains. Oishii is a parent or grandparent of 9.
  • Closest pairs: Ichibo x Oishii, Ichibo x Lemon Venom, Moonbow 112 IX x Oishii (about 75% shared).
  • Best outcrosses for the Moonbow-heavy group: any non-Archive strain above, or Nu Walka / Wraith (about 9% Moonbow).

Caveat: Nu Diesel, DogWalker OG, Super Runtz, Albert Walker, Lemon Peel, I-95, Medi Haze, Tuna Kush, Poison Potion, Kodiak Gold, Herijuana, Lamb's Bread, The Wife and Charlotte's Cherries have no published parents, so hidden overlap is possible. Special D's and Texas Butter F2 have no lineage in your notes yet, so they are not part of any comparison above.
